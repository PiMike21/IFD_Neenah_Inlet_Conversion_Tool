Calculation basis

Q = (0.56 / n) × (1 / ST) × D8/3 × √SL
Qc = min(Q, K × D5/3)    |    Qb = max(0, Q − Qc)

Catalog labels and basis K values are derived from the source manual extraction. Custom calculations are limited to a uniform triangular gutter, so road and gutter cross slopes must be equal. When slopes differ from the catalog basis, provide a Neenah K coefficient verified for those slopes. The downloaded CSV contains only “Approach Flow” and “Captured Flow,” matching the InfoDrainage rated-by-flow import structure.
